Local services Sample directory

Examples of services residents may find through a barangay. Requirements and availability differ by location.

  • 01

    Barangay clearance

    Ask your barangay office about applying for a clearance and which identification or supporting documents to bring.

    ↗
  • 02

    Certificates of residency

    Inquire locally about proof-of-residency certificates, eligibility, processing time, and any applicable fees.

    ↗
  • 03

    Community mediation

    Learn whether a local lupon or barangay mediation service can help with an eligible neighborhood dispute.

    ↗
  • 04

    Health and social support

    Ask the barangay health center or local office about available referrals, schedules, and community support programs.

    ↗
  • 05

    Certificate of indigency inquiry

    Ask the local barangay office whether this certificate is available and confirm any current requirements directly with staff.

    ↗
  • 06

    Disaster preparedness information

    Inquire about local preparedness guidance, emergency contacts, and community safety information for your area.

    ↗
  • 07

    Community health referrals

    Ask where to find local health services or referrals and verify schedules and availability with the relevant office.

    ↗

This directory is illustrative only and is not a list of confirmed services or official requirements.
